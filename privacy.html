Privacy Policy

Mechanic Cert Prep (“Mech Prep”) · Essential Dev Team
Effective date: September 15, 2026

The short version: Mechanic Cert Prep does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no servers of ours, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your study progress stays on your iPhone.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mechanic Cert Prep iOS app (the “App”), published by Essential Dev Team (“we”, “us”), handles information. It is written to meet the transparency expectations of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA), and similar laws.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, the App:

  • has no user accounts, registration or sign-in;
  • does not run on, or send your data to, any server operated by us or on our behalf;
  • contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution SDKs, and no other third-party SDKs;
  • does not show ads and does not track you across apps or websites, and does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission;
  • does not access your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, the App saves the following only on your device:

  • study progress and answer history;
  • bookmarked questions;
  • flashcard progress;
  • your study plan and app preferences (such as reminder settings).

This data lives in the App’s private storage container and in an App Group container shared only with the App’s own home-screen widget, so the widget can display your progress. It is never transmitted to us. We cannot see, access, recover or delete it on your behalf.

If your device’s iCloud or computer backup is turned on, iOS may include this App data in your device backup. Those backups are handled by Apple (or your computer) under your own settings and Apple’s privacy policy; we have no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can erase all study data at any time with Settings → Reset All Progress inside the App. Deleting the App from your iPhone removes all of its data, including the widget’s shared data.

3. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases of Mechanic Cert Prep Pro (weekly or yearly subscriptions, or a lifetime purchase) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never receive your name, email address, payment card or billing details. The App asks Apple only whether your Apple ID currently has an active purchase, so it can unlock Pro features. Apple’s handling of payment information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

The App offers optional daily study reminders. These are local notifications scheduled on your device — they are not sent from a server, and no push token is created or shared. The App asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on. You can turn reminders off in the App or in iOS Settings → Notifications at any time.

5. App update check

When the App launches, it contacts Apple’s public iTunes Lookup API (itunes.apple.com) with the App’s bundle identifier to check whether a newer version is available on the App Store. The request contains no personal data, account identifiers or study data. Like any internet request, it is received by Apple’s servers, which see your device’s IP address as a normal part of network communication; we do not receive or log it.

6. App Store privacy label

Consistent with the above, the App’s App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”: the developer does not collect any data from this app.

7. Legal bases and your rights

Because we do not collect or process personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, port, restrict, delete or “sell/share”. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA, and we do not use personal information for targeted advertising or profiling. You remain in full control of the data on your device as described in section 2. If you have a question about your rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

The App is designed for adults and students preparing for automotive technician certification. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Your data stays within iOS’s sandboxed app storage, protected by your device’s passcode and iOS data protection. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no server-side copy that could be exposed.

10. Emails you send us

If you choose to email our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and do not add it to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect in the App. Material changes will also be reflected in the App’s App Store privacy label.

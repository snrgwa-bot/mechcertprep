FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this app affiliated with ASE?

No. Mechanic Cert Prep is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence (ASE). ASE is a trademark of its owner.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Every question is written originally for the app to help you practice the published content areas. It does not reproduce actual exam content.

Which tests are covered?

A1 Engine Repair, A2 Automatic Transmission/Transaxle, A3 Manual Drive Train & Axles, A4 Suspension & Steering, A5 Brakes, A6 Electrical/Electronic Systems, A7 Heating & Air Conditioning, A8 Engine Performance, A9 Light Vehicle Diesel Engines, G1 Auto Maintenance & Light Repair, and L1 Advanced Engine Performance.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your progress is stored only on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Questions, flashcards, the study guide and diagrams all work without a connection.

Does it guarantee I'll pass?

No app can guarantee a result. Readiness scores are estimates based on your practice, meant to help you decide where to study.

What languages does it support?

English and Spanish.

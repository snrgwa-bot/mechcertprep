Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App, go to Settings (or the Pro screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are tied to your Apple ID, not to an account with us.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Mechanic Cert Prep and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period; you keep access until the current period ends. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I reset my progress?

In the App go to Settings → Reset All Progress. This permanently erases your answer history, bookmarks, flashcard progress and study plan on this device. It does not affect your purchases. Because your data is stored only on your device, we cannot restore it after a reset.

My daily reminders aren’t showing up

  • Check that reminders are turned on in the App’s Settings and a time is set.
  • Open iOS Settings → Notifications → Mech Prep and make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  • Check that a Focus mode or Scheduled Summary isn’t holding the notification.
  • If you declined permission earlier, enable it in iOS Settings, then toggle reminders off and on in the App.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, tap Edit → Add Widget (or the + button), search for Mech Prep, pick the Study Progress widget, choose a size and tap Add Widget. Open the App once after installing so the widget has progress to show.

Which tests are covered?

A1 Engine Repair, A2 Automatic Transmission/Transaxle, A3 Manual Drive Train & Axles, A4 Suspension & Steering, A5 Brakes, A6 Electrical/Electronic Systems, A7 Heating & Air Conditioning, A8 Engine Performance, A9 Light Vehicle Diesel Engines, G1 Auto Maintenance & Light Repair, and L1 Advanced Engine Performance — 11 tests in total.

Is the App affiliated with ASE?

No. Mechanic Cert Prep is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence (ASE). All questions are original and do not reproduce actual exam content.

What languages does the App support?

English and Spanish. The App follows your iPhone’s language setting.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed, and study features work offline. See our Privacy Policy for details.
